How scoring works
  • A board counts as solved when every pipe carries water, not just the houses.
  • Each board is worth its size × 10 points: 5×5 = 250.
  • Stars multiply that: ★★★ ×3, ★★ ×2, ★ ×1.
  • ★★★ means solved in the fewest possible taps. ★★ allows 50% more.
  • The bar at the top shows your stars live and how many taps you have left to keep them.
  • Each hint costs 25% of that board's points.
  • Boards grow as you win, and the run never ends.

    How to play

    1. 👆
      Tap a tile to rotate it.
      Right-click turns it the other way.
    2. 🏠
      Get water to every house.
      Water flows from the tap: the round tile with the blue drop 💧.
    3. 💧
      Use every pipe.
      The level is solved only when every pipe is full of water and no pipe end is left open. Watering the houses alone is not enough.
